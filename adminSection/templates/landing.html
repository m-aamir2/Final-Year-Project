How FYPAS Works

1
Find Available Supervisor

Explore and select a supervisor for your final year project based on their expertise and availability on FYPAS.

2
Submit Your Proposal

Submit your project proposal. Ensure accuracy, as proposals cannot be edited after submission.

3
Supervisor Approval

Your chosen supervisor reviews and approves your proposal, which is then forwarded to the coordinator for final approval.

4
Start Your Project

Once fully approved, begin working on your project, schedule meetings, and track progress through FYPAS.
